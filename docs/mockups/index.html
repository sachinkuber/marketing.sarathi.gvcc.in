The product, screen by screen

A clickable mockup of the dashboard at marketing.sarathi.gvcc.in. It shows what each screen looks like and how you move between them. Nothing here is connected to anything, and every number, name and message is made up.

Start with Home

Home leads with results and progress, then what is waiting for you, then problems. From there the menu on the left reaches everything else.

Use the Mockup controls

The button at the bottom left of each screen switches who you are viewing as, turns on the kill switch or shadow mode, shows the overdue banner and pop-up, and narrows the page to phone width.

Read the phase tags

Each screen is tagged with the phase that builds it. Phase 1 is the foundation. The rest arrive in phases 2 to 5, after the pilot, or when the product is sold.
