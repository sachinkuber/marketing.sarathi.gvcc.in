How the product works, start to finish

The agents do the work. You decide what goes live. This page follows the main journeys step by step. Each card opens the screen where that step happens. Cards with a blue edge are the steps where a person has to act.

1. Learn the brand

Research the website and channels, ask a tailored questionnaire, confirm facts, write the strategy brief.

2. Plan

Turn the brief into campaigns and a weekly content calendar.

3. Make

Agents write, design and check each item against safety rules and quality scores.

4. You approve

Nothing goes live without a decision. Silence never approves. Late items escalate and show as overdue.

5. Publish and learn

Post, track leads and results, report, and feed what worked into the next plan.
